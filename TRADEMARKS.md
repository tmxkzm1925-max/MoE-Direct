# MoE-Direct name & branding policy

The source code in this repository is licensed under the MIT License — you may
use, modify and redistribute it freely under that license.

The **MoE-Direct name and logo are a separate matter**. They identify this
project, its official repository (`github.com/tmxkzm1925-max/moe-direct`) and
its official releases. To keep that meaning intact:

**You may**, without asking:
- state truthfully that your software uses, is based on, or is compatible with
  MoE-Direct (e.g. "based on MoE-Direct", "MoE-Direct-compatible");
- package unmodified official releases under the MoE-Direct name with a link
  back to this repository.

**Please do not**:
- distribute a modified version under the plain name "MoE-Direct" or a
  confusingly similar name, or in a way that implies it is an official build or
  that it is endorsed by the author — name your fork clearly as your own
  (e.g. "YourProject, a fork of MoE-Direct");
- use the MoE-Direct name or logo as the primary branding of a commercial
  product or service without permission.

This policy restricts branding only, never the code. Questions or permission
requests: open an issue in this repository.
