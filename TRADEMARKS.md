# NVMoE name & branding policy

The source code in this repository is licensed under the MIT License — you may
use, modify and redistribute it freely under that license.

The **NVMoE name and logo are a separate matter**. They identify this project,
its official repository (`github.com/tmxkzm1925-max/nvmoe`) and its official
releases. To keep that meaning intact:

**You may**, without asking:
- state truthfully that your software uses, is based on, or is compatible with
  NVMoE (e.g. "based on NVMoE", "NVMoE-compatible");
- package unmodified official releases under the NVMoE name with a link back
  to this repository.

**Please do not**:
- distribute a modified version under the plain name "NVMoE" or a confusingly
  similar name, or in a way that implies it is an official build or that it is
  endorsed by the author — name your fork clearly as your own
  (e.g. "YourProject, a fork of NVMoE");
- use the NVMoE name or logo as the primary branding of a commercial product
  or service without permission.

This policy restricts branding only, never the code. Questions or permission
requests: open an issue in this repository.
